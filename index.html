Envida Ads Dashboard

Envida provides analytics and reporting for client TikTok Ad Accounts that are managed through the Envida Social Business Center. Clients grant Envida permission inside TikTok Business Center, and our system retrieves performance and advertising data on their behalf to display in their Envida dashboard.

This website also includes a TikTok OAuth Demo page required for TikTok App Review. The Login Kit flow shown on this page is used only for verifying the EnvidaTikTokAds integration and does not authenticate consumer TikTok users.

Data Usage

Envida uses TikTok’s APIs solely to access authorized advertiser data for analytics and reporting. We do not collect or store personal TikTok user data. All data access is permission-based and managed through TikTok Business Center.
