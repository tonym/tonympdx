Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

This website is used solely to demonstrate TikTok OAuth Login flow for TikTok App Review. It is not a commercial service and does not create user accounts.

Information We Receive

When you click “Login with TikTok,” TikTok may redirect your browser back to this site with an OAuth authorization code. This code is displayed on-screen only for review verification and is not stored, logged, or transmitted anywhere.

As part of TikTok’s Login Kit, TikTok may also make available basic profile information associated with the scope user.info.basic (including open_id, avatar URL, and display name). This site does not access, capture, or store that information.

No Data Storage or Processing

We do not collect, store, retain, process, or share any personal information obtained during the OAuth demonstration.

No Cookies or Tracking

This site does not set cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party trackers.

Data Sharing

No information is shared with third parties. No TikTok information is transmitted to servers or external systems.

Security

Because no data is stored, no additional security measures are required. The site is served statically over HTTPS.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to comply with TikTok App Review requirements.
